Emotional Toolkit

Quick practices you can tap while we talk.

2-minute grounding

Name 5 things you can see, 4 you can feel, 3 you can hear, 2 you can smell, and 1 you can taste. Go slowly. You can share what you notice here.

Calm breathing

Breathe in for 4, hold for 4, out for 6. Repeat a few times. Notice where your body softens, even a little.

Self-kindness reframe

If a close friend felt like you do, what would you want them to hear? You can ask the bot to help put this into words.

Shared Stories

Add short anonymous stories about moments you struggled and what (if anything) helped. They stay in your browser and help the bot share more human examples back with you.

Stories are stored privately in your browser and used to make responses feel more human.

Using OpenRouter with EmoteAI

Online replies use your own OpenRouter API key. You stay in control of the key, and this page only sends it directly to OpenRouter.

  1. Go to openrouter.ai and sign in or create an account.
  2. In your OpenRouter dashboard, find the API Keys section and create a new key.
  3. Copy the key (it usually starts with sk-or- ). Keep it secret and do not share it publicly.
  4. Paste the key into the OpenRouter API key box at the top of this page. The app will remember it only in this browser.
  5. Click AI mode: Online (OpenRouter) to switch from offline local replies to online model replies.

If a key is ever exposed or shared by accident, you can go back to OpenRouter, delete that key, and create a new one.
